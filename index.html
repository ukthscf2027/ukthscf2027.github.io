About the Forum

Shaping the Future of Smart Cities

The UK-Thailand Smart City Forum 2027 brings together leading researchers, policymakers, and industry experts from the United Kingdom and Thailand to exchange insights on the most pressing challenges and innovations in smart city development.

This is an invited-speaker forum — distinguished experts present their latest work through pre-recorded video presentations available online and live online talks, fostering focused, high-quality discussion across both nations.

Supported by the IET (Institution of Engineering and Technology) and associated with the IET Smart Cities journal, the forum bridges academic research with real-world urban innovation.

Presentation Format

How the Forum Works

An invited-speaker forum with flexible presentation options

🎥

Video Presentations

Invited speakers provide pre-recorded video presentations that are made available online for all attendees to view at their convenience.

📡

Live Online Talks

Speakers can also present live online during the forum day, with real-time Q&A and interactive discussion with the audience.

💬

Panel Discussions

Interactive panel sessions bring together experts from the UK and Thailand for cross-national dialogue on smart city challenges and collaboration.

This is an invited-speaker forum. There is no open call for papers.
