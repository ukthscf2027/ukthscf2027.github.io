About the Forum

Shaping the Future of Smart Cities

The UK-Thailand Smart City Forum 2027 brings together researchers, policymakers, industry leaders, and innovators from both nations to address the most pressing challenges in urban technology and smart city development.

Building on the strong bilateral partnership between the United Kingdom and Thailand, this forum provides a unique platform for sharing research, best practices, and innovative solutions in smart city technologies — from IoT and AI-driven infrastructure to sustainable urban planning and digital governance.

The forum aims to foster collaboration that translates into real-world impact, connecting academic research with industry application and policy development.

Call for Papers

We invite original research contributions on all aspects of smart cities

Submission Guidelines

Authors are invited to submit original, unpublished research papers that address challenges and innovations in smart city development. Submissions will be peer-reviewed by an international programme committee.

  • Full papers: up to 16 pages (including references)
  • Short papers: up to 8 pages (including references)
  • Extended abstracts: up to 4 pages
  • Formatting: follow the provided template (details coming soon)

Accepted papers will be included in the forum proceedings. Selected papers may be invited for publication in a special issue of a partnering journal.
